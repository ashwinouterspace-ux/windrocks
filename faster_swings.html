Faster Swings Records

Rules

Faster Swings is a record game where you have to get your eyes over the top part of the swings (where the chains hang from) as fast as possible.

Main area

Buffalo Ridge Park


The dumi_bum record for Faster Swings is held by Prithvi Sivaraman with a time of 10.72!
