Post-It Bouncer

Setup

To play Post-It Bouncer, you need these things:

  • At least 3 square Post-Its
  • A flat surface
  • A friend
  • A notepad

Take the Post-Its, flip them to the back, and fold them so that all the corners reach the middle. Unfold them halfway. Stack the Post-Its and fold the smaller corners.

How to Play

Start bouncing the combined Post-It and try to keep bouncing it. Ask your friend to count the bounces. After the combined Post-It falls on the ground, ask your friend for the number of bounces. Write it down.
